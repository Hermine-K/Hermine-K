## About

From a clinical laboratory in Benin to large-scale data science in France, my work follows one idea: understand complex systems and make them usable. I currently build an AI segmentation pipeline for whole-cell SBF-SEM electron microscopy images at the CBS (CNRS). My interests span biomedical imaging, genomics and molecular dynamics, combining machine learning, deep learning and data engineering to turn fuzzy, large-scale problems into concrete solutions.
